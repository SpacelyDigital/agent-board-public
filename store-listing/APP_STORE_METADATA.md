## Description

Agent Board keeps your active coding-agent chats in one local Mac dashboard.
It supports the Codex Mac app and Claude Desktop’s Code tab at their standard
local storage locations. Those provider apps and their own accounts are separate.

Check which chats are working, need your input or have a reply ready. Open the
original chat, mark a reply reviewed, or restore it from dashboard history.
New activity returns automatically. Internal workers and older background
sessions stay separate from your main board.

Choose among five layouts and fifteen themes with light and dark modes.
Enlarge text from the native View menu, keep the board on top, or close its
window and keep monitoring from the menu bar. Choose and preview one of ten
local alert sounds, or turn sounds off. Native notifications are optional.

Local usage panels can show Claude’s five-hour and weekly windows and Codex’s
weekly window, with reset times when available. These are cached observations
from the coding tools. They may be absent or stale and do not verify the current
account or a fresh allowance. An optional Claude plugin exports its quota
readings locally; Agent Board does not send prompts to update them.

With your permission, Agent Board reads selected local source folders. Board
state, preferences and logs stay on your Mac. There is no Agent Board account,
developer reporting endpoint, analytics SDK, advertising tracker or crash
uploader. Original chats are not edited and board data is not uploaded to the
developer. Browser-only and unrelated provider conversations are outside scope.
Provider formats can change, and unconfirmed states remain clearly unavailable.

Agent Board is free. Eligible US storefronts may show an optional Ko-fi link
and local QR in About after native Apple storefront checks. Donations unlock
nothing. Choosing to visit or donate through Ko-fi, or emailing support, uses
those services and can share your chosen contact/message/transaction details
with Spacely Digital. Agent Board attaches no board data automatically.

Agent Board is independent of OpenAI and Anthropic. Their apps and services
operate under their own terms and privacy policies.
